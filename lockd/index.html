Lockd: Daily Motivation

Eyes open. Locked in. Short, original motivational quotes on your home screen, lock screen and in a swipeable feed. No accounts, no ads. Requires a Lockd Premium purchase.

FAQ

How do I add the widget to my home screen?

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap the + button in the top corner.
  3. Search for "Lockd" and choose a small or medium widget.
  4. Tap Add Widget, then Done.

How do I add the widget to my lock screen?

  1. Touch and hold your lock screen, then tap Customize and choose Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the area under the clock (rectangular widgets) or the line above the clock (inline widget).
  3. Search for "Lockd" and pick the rectangular or inline widget.
  4. Tap Done.

The widget shows a new quote about every hour, drawn from your chosen topics. It uses the theme and font you apply in the app.

How do I change my topics?

In the feed, tap the grid icon at the top right to open Topics. Tap any topic to add or remove it from your mix. Your feed and widgets update right away. If you clear every topic, Lockd shows quotes from all topics.

How do reminders work?

Tap the bell icon to open Reminders. Choose how many quotes you want per day, a start and end time, and which days of the week. Tap Save reminders and allow notifications when iOS asks. Lockd then schedules local notifications on your device, spread evenly between your start and end time. Nothing is sent from a server.

To change your reminders, adjust the settings and save again. This replaces the previous schedule. To stop them, deselect every day and save, or turn off notifications for Lockd in iOS Settings.

How do I remove saved quotes?

Tap the bookmark icon on a quote to save it, and tap it again to unsave. To manage everything at once, open the bookmark icon at the top of the feed to see your saved quotes, then swipe left on a quote and tap Delete.

Can I change the look of the app and widget?

Yes. Tap the palette icon to pick a theme and font, then tap Apply. The widgets use the same choice.

Does Lockd need an account or internet?

No account is needed. All quotes are built into the app. An internet connection is needed to load the purchase screen and to buy or restore a plan. Your topics, saved quotes and settings are stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

How much does Lockd cost?

Lockd requires Lockd Premium: $19.99 per year, $4.99 per month, or a $39.99 one-time lifetime purchase. Prices may vary by region. There is no free tier and no free trial.

How do I cancel or restore my purchase?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, to cancel or change a plan. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen, or open Reminders in Lockd (bell icon) and scroll to the bottom.
