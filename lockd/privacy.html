Privacy Policy

Lockd: Daily Motivation · Effective date: November 25, 2026

Lockd is built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what stays on your device, and what is sent off it so that purchases work.

Summary

Your quotes, topics, saved items and settings stay on your device. Lockd has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Lockd is a paid app, so purchase information and some anonymous device information are processed by Apple and by Superwall, the service that runs the purchase screen.

What stays on your device

To work, Lockd saves a few preferences locally on your iPhone: the topics you selected, the quotes you saved, your theme and font, your reminder settings, your Quote Pause settings and counts, and whether Lockd Premium is active. These are kept in storage shared only between the Lockd app and its own widget on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

Lockd requires a Lockd Premium subscription or lifetime purchase. Payments are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. Lockd never sees your payment details, your name or your email address from Apple.

What is sent to Superwall

Lockd uses the Superwall SDK to show the onboarding and purchase screens and to check whether your purchase is active. When you use the app, Superwall receives:

  • Purchase information: which plan you bought or restored, and whether it is active.
  • Identifiers: a random user ID created by the SDK and your device's vendor identifier. Neither contains your name, email or Apple Account.
  • Device and app information: device model, iOS version, language, region, currency, time zone, app version and install date.
  • Approximate location: country, region and city estimated from your IP address. Lockd never asks for or uses your precise location.
  • Purchase screen activity: when the purchase screen was shown and what you tapped on it.
  • Your onboarding answers: the goal and struggle you pick, and your topic, reminder and theme choices. A first name you type during onboarding stays on your device and is not sent.

This information is used to run the purchase screen, unlock the app after a purchase, and understand how the purchase screen performs. It is not used for advertising and is not sold. Superwall handles it under its own privacy policy.

No accounts, ads or tracking

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers are used.
  • Lockd does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • All quotes are bundled in the app. The only network requests are the ones described above.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Lockd asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are local notifications scheduled and delivered by your device. No push notification server is involved and no notification token is collected. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings.

Quote Pause

Quote Pause runs only when a Shortcuts automation that you create starts it. Lockd does not monitor, read or block other apps. The apps you choose and your pause counts are stored on your device only.

Sharing

If you use the share button on a quote, the text of that quote is passed to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Lockd does not see or control the destination.

Third parties

Lockd uses two third parties: Apple, which processes payments, and Superwall, as described above. No data is shared with anyone else.

Your choices

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. To ask for the information Superwall holds about your device to be deleted, email the address below.

Children's privacy

Lockd is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app contains no ads, accounts or social features.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
