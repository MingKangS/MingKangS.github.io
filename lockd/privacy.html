Privacy Policy

Lockd: Daily Motivation · Effective date: November 14, 2026

Lockd is built to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Summary

Lockd does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What stays on your device

To work, Lockd saves a few preferences locally on your iPhone: the topics you selected, the quotes you saved, your theme and font, and your reminder settings. These are kept in storage shared only between the Lockd app and its own widget on your device. We never receive this information and cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools are included.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers are used.
  • Lockd does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Lockd makes no network requests. All quotes are bundled in the app.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Lockd asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are local notifications scheduled and delivered by your device. No push notification server is involved and no notification token is collected. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings.

Sharing

If you use the share button on a quote, the text of that quote is passed to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Lockd does not see or control the destination.

Third parties

Lockd does not use third-party SDKs or services, and no data is shared with third parties.

Children's privacy

Lockd does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app contains no ads, accounts or social features.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
